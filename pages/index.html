During real internet outages, Jellyfin has been used successfully on phones, TVs, and notebooks. This is owner-reported operating experience, not a controlled benchmark of every application.Owner clarification, November 2, 2026 (S0). Dates and durations of outages were not recorded.
What the outage reports don’t tell us
  • Exact dates or durations
  • How the WAN was lost
  • Client apps or OS versions
  • Where the played media lived
  • Direct play or transcoding
  • Concurrent streams
  • Existing session or fresh login
  • Restarts of host, NAS or router
  • Local DNS, cached DNS, or direct IP
  • Whether mobile data was off
  • Power or UPS behavior
  • Cold-start recovery

“Not established” means the material doesn’t support a result. It doesn’t mean the workflow failed.
